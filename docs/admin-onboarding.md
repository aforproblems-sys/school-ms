# Platform & School Administrator Onboarding — EduManage Pro

## 1. Platform Super Admin Responsibilities

The Platform Super Admin manages global SaaS operations across all school tenants:

- **School Tenant Management**: Create, suspend, or upgrade school accounts (`/dashboard/super-admin/schools`).
- **Subscription Governance**: Manage SaaS plans (`FREE_TRIAL`, `BASIC`, `PROFESSIONAL`, `ENTERPRISE`) and student/teacher quota caps (`/dashboard/super-admin/billing`).
- **System Health & Maintenance**: Toggle system maintenance mode and monitor database health latency (`/dashboard/super-admin/system`).
- **Global Audit Logs**: Review security events across all tenants (`/dashboard/super-admin/logs`).

---

## 2. School Admin Workflow & Management

The School Admin governs daily operations for their assigned school:

1. **Daily Overview**: View active student/teacher counts, attendance rates, and pending fee collections (`/dashboard/school-admin`).
2. **Staff & Student Management**: Enroll students, assign class sections, and manage staff user accounts (`/dashboard/users/students`).
3. **Attendance & Timetable**: Monitor attendance sheets and resolve schedule conflicts.
4. **Exams & Grading**: Approve exam schedules, review mark entry, and publish results.
5. **Financial Oversight**: Generate fee invoices, record payment receipts, and track overdue accounts (`/dashboard/finance/fees`).
